VESPER cheat sheet · M-VAVE FM-1 · Fat Cat Studios

An ambient worship synth: 16 scenes, four voices each, always in a major key. Press PLAY and play along.

Getting around

HOMEThe crystal orbit. Press again for the 4-track mixer.
PRESETSThe 16 scenes.
SEL + PRESETSYour saved user presets.
PLAYStart or stop the scene.
ARPKeys play the focused voice live (green PLAY badge). Press again: keys set the key.
RECWith ARP on, record what you play into the sequence.
SAVESave to one of 16 user slots, with sounds, FX and SPACE. K1 moves the cursor and K2 changes the letter to rename it.

On HOME

K1Key (major keys).
K2SPACE: one knob for grains, shimmer, reverb, echo and a cleaner low end.
K3Level of the focused layer.
K4BPM.
ALGORITHMWhich of the four voices you are on.

Shaping a voice

EDITFive pages per voice: SOUND, TONE, OSC, VOICE, MIX (level, pan, reverb send, echo send).
ENVAmp envelope; press again for the filter envelope.
LFOTempo-synced filter motion, autopan, analog drift.
SEL (hold)Every knob moves one fine step per click. On the mixer, the faders become pan.

Global effects · FX

FX ×1 · DIRTDrive, bits (16 down to 4), sample rate, mix.
FX ×2 · TONEHigh pass, low pass, a gentle compressor, output.
FX ×3 · TEXTUREGranular cloud (in time), octave shimmer, width, chorus.
FX ×4 · VERB + DELAYPlate reverb and tape-style stereo echo.

Settings · GLO

GLOClock and MIDI, swing, click, and THEME (Crystal, Ember, Forest, Mono). SELECT scrolls; ALGORITHM or K1 changes the value.

Install, update or go back to the stock firmware: the latest release. If the FM-1 ever will not start, hold REC while switching it on.
